FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about exporting with UA Imports & Exports.

Products & Sourcing

What products do you export?

We export a wide range of South Indian agro produce including fresh fruits (Alphonso Mangoes, King Coconuts, Pineapples, Bananas, Rambutan), vegetables (Drumsticks, Okra, Bitter Gourd, Eggplant), roots & tubers (Cassava, Turmeric Root, Shallots, Sweet Potato), and spices & others (Black Pepper, Cardamom, Nutmeg, Sugarcane).

Where do you source your produce from?

We have farms of our own across South India and a strong network of trusted partner growers — primarily in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh. If a particular item isn't produced on our own farms, we tap into our network to source it, ensuring traceability, fair pricing, and consistent quality either way.

Can you source products not listed on your website?

Yes. Our list is representational — if you have specific crop, variety, volume, or seasonal requirements, we can source and fulfil custom orders. Contact us with your requirements.

What volumes do you require for an order?

We work with both full-container loads and consolidated shipments. Minimum volumes vary by product and season — contact us and we'll advise on what works for your market.

Quality & Certifications

What quality certifications do you hold?

We are FSSAI licensed and APEDA registered. Our facilities follow GMP and GHP protocols. We also hold certifications from the Spices Board India, IOPEPC, MPEDA, and the Cashew Export Promotion Council of India.

Do you provide lab reports with shipments?

Yes. Lab-tested freshness, pesticide residue analysis, and microbiological safety reports are provided with every shipment as per buyer and destination country requirements.

How do you ensure freshness during transit?

We use farm-aligned scheduling, temperature-controlled handling, moisture-control packaging, and rapid port dispatch to minimize the time from harvest to delivery.

Can I request samples before placing an order?

Absolutely. We offer samples with full quality documentation. Sample costs and logistics are discussed based on your location and volume interest.

Orders & Logistics

What is the typical lead time for an order?

Lead times vary by product and season. Typically 7–14 days from order confirmation to dispatch for most products. Rush orders can be accommodated.

Which ports do you ship from?

We primarily ship from the major South Indian ports of Chennai, Cochin (Kochi), and Tuticorin — providing strategic access to international shipping routes.

What shipping methods do you offer?

We offer sea freight (reefer containers), air freight for time-sensitive orders, and multi-modal combinations depending on destination, volume, and budget.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. We provide real-time tracking updates at every stage — from farm loading and port arrival to vessel departure and destination clearance.

Payments & Customisation

What payment terms do you accept?

We accept TT (Telegraphic Transfer), LC (Letter of Credit), and other mutually agreed payment terms. Terms are discussed during order confirmation.

Do you offer custom packaging?

Yes. We offer custom export packaging including branded boxes, vacuum packing, modified atmosphere packaging (MAP), and buyer-specific labeling.

Can you handle documentation and customs clearance?

Fully. We manage all export documentation including phytosanitary certificates, certificate of origin, bill of lading, packing list, and customs clearance on the Indian end.

What Incoterms do you support?

We support FOB, CIF, CFR, EXW, and DAP depending on buyer preference and destination requirements. We'll help you choose the best option.
