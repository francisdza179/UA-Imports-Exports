Legal

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2025

1. Introduction

UA Imports & Exports ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or engage our agro-export services.

By using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please refrain from using our site or providing your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Contact details: Full name, email address, phone number, company name, and country of residence.
  • Business information: Import/export licenses, trade references, and shipping addresses provided during onboarding or inquiry.
  • Correspondence: Any messages, enquiries, or feedback you submit via our contact forms, email, or WhatsApp.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on site, referring URL, browser type, and device information collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • IP address: Used for analytics, security, and geographic routing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your product enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To manage our trade relationships, including order processing, shipping, and documentation.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including export documentation and customs requirements.
  • To send occasional service-related communications (you may opt out at any time).

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / UAE Law)

We process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: You have given clear consent for us to process your data for a specific purpose.
  • Contractual necessity: Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you (e.g., fulfilling an export order).
  • Legal obligation: We are required to process data to comply with applicable laws (e.g., customs and trade regulations).
  • Legitimate interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these do not override your rights and freedoms.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third-party logistics partners, freight forwarders, customs brokers, and payment processors who assist in fulfilling your orders.
  • Regulatory authorities: Government agencies and customs authorities as required by export/import laws.
  • Professional advisors: Lawyers, accountants, and auditors where necessary for compliance or dispute resolution.

All third-party providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may only use it for the specific services they provide on our behalf.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Trade-related records may be retained for up to 5 years to comply with customs and tax obligations. Enquiry records are retained for 2 years after the last contact.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Secure sockets layer (SSL) encryption on our website.
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular security audits and employee data protection training.

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data where it is no longer required for the purposes collected.
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing under certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and support our marketing efforts. You may control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Essential cookies are required for the basic functionality of our site; non-essential cookies (analytics, marketing) are used only with your consent.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects anonymised data such as pages visited and time on site. You can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

10. International Data Transfers

As an export business operating between India and the UAE, your personal data may be transferred across international borders. We ensure that appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are in place to protect your data when transferred to countries with different data protection laws.
