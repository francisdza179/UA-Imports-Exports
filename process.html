Rigorous Testing

Every Export Is Lab-Tested For Quality & Safety

Before any shipment leaves our facility, representative samples undergo comprehensive laboratory analysis — testing for pesticide residues, microbial contaminants, moisture content, and physical quality parameters. Our in-house agri testing lab works in tandem with accredited third-party facilities to ensure every export meets the stringent food safety standards of destination markets.

From phytosanitary certification to heavy metal screening, each parameter is verified against the requirements of FSSAI, APEDA, Spices Board India, and international importing regulations. This dedication to testing is what makes our produce trusted across 130+ countries.

How an international buyer sources premium Indian agro produce through us — from first inquiry to delivery at your warehouse.

1

Send us your requirement

Day 1

Tell us what you need — produce type, volume, destination country, packaging preference, and delivery timeline. We respond within 24 hours with availability and indicative pricing.

WhatsApp / email Product catalogue available 24-hour response
2

Sample and quality approval

Day 3–7

We dispatch physical samples of the produce with full quality reports — grade, moisture content, pesticide residue levels, shelf life, and lab certifications. You approve before any bulk order is placed.

FSSAI certified APEDA approved Lab test reports included Phytosanitary cert
3

Confirm order and terms

Day 7–10

Once samples are approved, we issue a formal proforma invoice with quantity, price (FOB/CIF/CFR), Incoterms, payment terms, and packaging specs. We accept LC, TT advance, or escrow for first orders.

FOB / CIF / CFR LC accepted TT advance Incoterms 2020
4

Sourcing, grading, and packing

Day 10–18

We source directly from our verified farmer network across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, and Punjab. Produce is graded, cleaned, treated, and packed to your specification — retail-ready, bulk, or private label.

Farm-direct sourcing Custom packaging Cold chain maintained Private label available
5

Customs & documentation

Day 18–22

We handle all Indian export compliance — APEDA registration, phytosanitary inspection, Certificate of Origin, shipping bill, packing list, and commercial invoice. Zero documentation burden on your end.

Certificate of Origin Phytosanitary cert Shipping bill APEDA export code
6

Shipment and live tracking

Day 22–45

Cargo departs from Chennai, Mumbai, or Mundra port depending on your destination. We share the Bill of Lading, container number, and vessel details immediately. Live shipment tracking available throughout transit.

Ongoing
