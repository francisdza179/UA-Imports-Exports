Who We Are

Where Quality Produce Meets
World-Class Logistics

We connect farms to global markets with premium, export-grade agro produce. From South India's finest Tender Coconuts and Cassava to Alphonso Mangoes, Drumsticks, and Shallots — we source, quality-check, package, and ship with care.

Our Heritage: Our journey began offline. For over two decades, our family managed agricultural trade and direct-farm sourcing across South India's fertile agricultural belts. In 2026, we founded UA Imports & Exports to build upon this deep-rooted network, bringing generations of local farming relationships and rigorous quality standards to the international digital marketplace.

We own and manage farms across South India's most fertile agricultural belts. Our hands are in the soil — overseeing cultivation from planting to harvest, working alongside local farming communities who bring generations of expertise to every crop. For produce we don't grow ourselves, our trusted network of partner farms ensures we never fall short.

Every shipment is backed by our family's 25-year sourcing legacy, FSSAI & APEDA compliance, and global shipping capabilities.

Our Source

Why South India?

Our roots run deep in South India — a region blessed with diverse climates, fertile soils, and generations of farming wisdom that make it one of the world's most productive agricultural belts.

Year-Round Growing Seasons

South India's tropical climate enables multiple harvest cycles per year — meaning consistent supply and fresher produce regardless of season.

Strategic Port Access

Major international ports in Chennai, Cochin, and Tuticorin put us within hours of loading — drastically reducing farm-to-ship transit time.

Generations of Agricultural Heritage

Farming isn't just an occupation here — it's a legacy. Generations of expertise translate into superior crop knowledge, quality, and consistency.

Diverse Agro-Climatic Zones

From coastal coconuts to highland spices and fertile delta vegetables — South India's varied micro-climates let us source an unmatched range of produce.
